# Career Market Strategy

Last updated: 2026-07-22

## Positioning

Baran's strongest route is not “generic junior coder.” It is:

> Industrial electrical maintenance experience + reliable software/systems engineering + Linux/networking + OT/ICS defense and resilience + secure, responsible AI integration.

## Primary destination

Secure industrial systems and OT/ICS cybersecurity. Target titles may include OT/ICS security analyst or engineer, secure industrial software/integration engineer, industrial IoT/product-security engineer, industrial incident-response/resilience specialist, or industrial AI assurance/integration engineer.

Industrial software and integration remain the strongest entry bridge. The goal is not to wait for one perfect job title before gaining relevant experience.

## Entry-bridge role families

1. Industrial software, manufacturing systems, MES/SCADA integration, and industrial data/API work.
2. Automation-software, test-systems, controls-support, applications, and solutions roles.
3. Technical support, product security, backend integration, and reliability roles for industrial/infrastructure vendors.
4. OT/ICS cybersecurity, monitoring, response/recovery, and resilience roles after software, Linux, networking, and industrial foundations are demonstrated.

## Calendar and certification

- The 12-month map is a Year 1 planning window, not a deadline.
- Accelerate or extend only through mastery evidence and verified market needs.
- Revisit entry certification at Week 39. Do not chase credentials before the underlying Linux, networking, software, and security skills exist.

## Adaptation rules

- Monthly: review authoritative labor, AI-at-work, industrial, cybersecurity, and materially relevant world evidence.
- Weeks 13, 26, 39, and 52: run a deep review using 20–30 current relevant postings.
- Change future emphasis only when two credible sources and the posting sample reveal the same meaningful gap.
- Require the signal across two deep reviews before a major rewrite, except urgent safety/legal/platform-breaking changes.
- Accelerate only through demonstrated mastery; remediate when explanations, predictions, transfer, or debugging show weak prerequisites.
- Never revise completed grades or mastery because the market changed.

## Portfolio evidence added to Year 1

- Month 3: README, sample I/O, tests, and debugging postmortem.
- Month 5: issue, feature branch, PR-style review, reproducible setup, and CI.
- Month 8: reliability case study.
- Month 9: maintenance-to-software troubleshooting story.
- Month 11: service health/observability concepts and operational runbook.
- Month 12: role-specific capstone case study, resume bullets, concise demo, technical walkthrough, and honest AI-use statement.

The capstone also includes defensive monitoring with invented events, an asset/trust-zone model, incident response/recovery evidence, and proof that AI remains advisory and cannot control or autonomously respond in the simulated industrial process.
